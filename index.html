Overview

Overview

This analysis evaluates three potential development sites: Poplar Point, Project Connect, and Tysons Central. Each site was assessed based on financial feasibility, sensitivity stress testing, and policy leverage.

While Project Connect and Tysons Central strengthen existing growth corridors, Poplar Point represents a different investment opportunity centered on waterfront activation, and long-term civic value.

Based on this analysis, Sanctuary at Poplar Point is selected as the preferred investment site due to its superior long-term policy leverage, catalytic development potential, and ability to fill an infrastructure gap in the region.

Sensitivity Analysis (Risk Exposure)

Real estate feasibility is margin-sensitive. Small changes in rents, costs, or cap rates significantly impact residual land value.

  • Project Connect: Maintains positive Residual Land Value under all stress scenarios, indicating strong downside protection due to transit adjacency and institutional anchoring.
  • Tysons Central: Highly sensitive to cap rate expansion and cost increases, reflecting suburban office exposure and retrofit complexity.
  • Poplar Point: Most sensitive to stress due to early-stage redevelopment conditions, not weak demand fundamentals.

Interpretation of Risk

Poplar Point’s negative stress outcomes do not indicate lack of demand—they reflect high upfront capital requirements required to activate a dormant and environmental constraint waterfront asset.

In contrast, Tysons and Project Connect derive stability from pre-existing infrastructure and established market ecosystems.

This distinction separates “optimization of existing value” from “creation of new urban value.”

Policy Value Comparison

    Policy leverage measures how effectively each site converts public investment and institutional support into long-term public value beyond financial return.

    • Poplar Point: Highest policy leverage. This site represents a rare opportunity to transform underutilized waterfront land into a civic and wellness focused destination. It directly supports District goals for waterfront access, public health, and spatial equity, particularly for communities east of the Anacostia river.
    • Project Connect: Moderate policy leverage. The project aligns with transit-oriented development and Maryland’s innovation economy strategy. However, it primarily strengthens an already established innovation corridor rather than addressing underserved areas or creating new civic infrastructure.
    • Tysons Central: Lower policy leverage. The project supports incremental suburban redevelopment and improved walkability but is constrained by existing auto-oriented infrastructure and a mature commercial market.

    Poplar Point is the strongest investment option because it addresses a broader unmet regional need, offers the strongest long-term upside, and aligns economic performance with public need and policy direction in a single site, despite higher upfront costs and lower near-term residual land value.

Final Recommendation

Poplar Point

Although Poplar Point has the lowest financial output, it delivers the highest long-term urban and public value.

Project Connect and Tysons optimize existing markets. Poplar Point creates a new one.
